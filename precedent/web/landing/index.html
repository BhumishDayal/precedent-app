How it works

Same model, same warning pattern. Precedent looks up what happened to every drive that was in exactly this spot before, then decides whether anyone should be woken up.

  1. Your fleet streams SMART readings

    A small collector reads every disk with smartctl and posts the counters. Or send them straight to the API.

    CollectorREST API

  2. Precedent counts every identical drive

    Same model plus same warning pattern, like 5+197+198:high. How many were there, and how many died within 30 days?

  3. When history says act, it calls the on-call

    A call or text with the evidence, not just an alarm.

    "59 drives like this, 26 died within 30 days."

  4. The decision is logged and agents act

    Swap or wait becomes part of the drive's history. Agents pick up the rest.

    Warranty searchRemote-hands ticket
